THEATRICAL MATERIALS
7 second A24 logo is required at the head and tail of the feature.
Ask your post/delivery contact for the correct aspect ratio logo.
Digital Cinema Packages
Electronic delivery (if physical, on an EXT3 formatted CRU DCP) of one encrypted multi-reel DCP with a single CPL adhering to the DCI specification for INTEROP and SMPTE DCPs. Your delivery contact can loop you in with Deluxe for Aspera upload link. Guidelines for Digital Cinema Source and DCP can be found here: Deluxe_Source-and-DCP_Delivery_Specifications_v5-11_20220314 2022.pdf
 DCI Compliant Digital Cinema Package (DCP) shall be created from the Digital Cinema Distribution Master (DCDM)
 The DCP must be in reels and must match the DCDM.
 PLEASE NOTE: Film reel maximum length = 20 minutes and 50 frames.
 A DKDM open for the Term must also be provided.
 If 2K image, must be encoded with a guardbit value of 1. 
 If 4K image, must be encoded with a guardbit value of 2.
 Studio code is “A24”
 Digital Cinema Naming Convention (DCNC): https://registry-page.isdcf.com/ 
INTEROP Digital Cinema Package (5.1/7.1)**
Physical delivery of one (1) INTEROP DCI Compliant 5.1 OV Digital Cinema Package (DCP) which shall be created from the Digital Cinema Distribution Master. 
 The DCP must be in reels and must match the DCDM.
 If 7.1 audio is available, an additional 7.1 VF DCP that points to the 5.1 OV ‘parent’ DCP should be provided.
 A DKDM must also be provided for all DCPs.
SMPTE Bv2.1 Digital Cinema Package (Dolby Atmos/7.1)**
Physical delivery of one (1) SMPTE Bv2.1 DCI Compliant Digital Cinema Package on a CRU drive which shall be created from the Digital Cinema Distribution Master. 
The SMPTE DCP must be in reels and must match the DCDM. 
Audio MXF shall utilize the “Configuration 4” Channel Assignment UL as noted in SMPTE ST 429-2:2020 [see: https://doi.org/10.5594/SMPTE.ST429-2.2020], commonly referred to as “WTF” or “Wild TrackFormat”. No other channel formats, including “Undefined” or “0” (IOP), nor other formats described in SMTPE ST 429-2:2020, will be accepted. 
If 7.1 is available, please include 7.1 track. 
A DKDM must also be provided for all DCPs.
Theatrical Timed Text Files
Interop DCP utilized Timed Text XML and any ancillary files must be delivered compliant to Texas Instruments DCinema Subtitle Specification V1.1 available at: http://www.deluxecdn.com/dcinema/reference/ti_subtitling_spec_v1_1.pdf
SMPTE DCP utilized (OR) 3D (for use in 3D subtitle compositing) Timed Text XML and any ancillary files must be delivered compliant to SMPTE ST 428-7:2014 DCDM Subtitle specification. See https://www.smpte.org/
Theatrical Subtitling Files (If Applicable)**
Physical delivery of one (1) electronic copy of the filmmaker-approved English subtitles as XML and TTF files in reels that are synced with the final DCP.
 Subtitle standard is 42 characters per line.
 English subtitling files are required if subtitles are present in the OV feature.
Theatrical Closed Caption Files (If Available)**
Physical delivery of one (1) electronic copy of the filmmaker-approved English SDH closed captions as XML and TTF files in reels that are synced with the final DCP. 
 Please note that a limit of 30 characters per line is a theatrical CC standard.
 English caption files are optional but should be delivered if previously available for festival screenings, etc.

D. ARCHIVE MATERIALS
PLEASE READ: Physical Delivery Requirements for Archival LTOs and/or Hard Drives
LTO Archives:
Once content is written to tape, please send your A24 delivery contact an .md5 checksum and either a photo of the final labelled tape or a .txt file of the final label information that will be included on the tape (label information can be found below).
Deliverables via Hard Drive:
Please send your A24 delivery contact screenshots of file structure and/or a full manifest of drive contents, .md5 checksum, and either a photo of the final labelled hard drive or a .txt file of the final label information that will be included on the drive (label information can be found below).
Your A24 delivery contact will review the information requested above and provide a shipping address for physical deliverables.
Digital Cinema Distribution Master 
Digital Cinema Distribution Master
Physical delivery of one (1) Digital Cinema Distribution Master of 16-bit XYZ TIFF files in reels and 24fps wav files written in TAR or LTFS format on LTO7 or LTO8. Each reel, including textless, should be written on a separate LTO.
This should include textless backgrounds to the main and end titles of the Picture, and anywhere else text appears over Picture.
Picture archival deliveries must include a checksum at the same level as the picture file sequence.
Textless Material Only Digital Cinema Distribution Master**
Physical delivery of one (1) Textless Material Only Digital Cinema Distribution Master of 16-bit XYZ TIFF files in 24fps delivered electronically to A24. 
This should include textless backgrounds to the main and end titles of the Picture, and anywhere else text appears over Picture. 
Textless background files should be provided in the order in which they appear in the film. Please provide cutting notes containing description, reel number, foot and frame or timecode for where text appears.
Graded Archival Master
Physical delivery of the P3 DCI 4K color corrected 16-bit DPX files in reels on LTO tape, written in LTFS format on LTO7 or LTO8. Each reel, including textless, should be written on a separate LTO. Picture archival deliveries must include a checksum at the same level as the picture file sequence.
Non-Graded Archival Master
Physical delivery of the 4K non-color corrected conformed 16-bit DPX files in reels on LTO tape, written in LTFS format on LTO7 or LTO8. Each reel, including textless, should be written on a separate LTO. Picture archival deliveries must include a checksum at the same level as the picture file sequence.
Dolby Vision Archival Master
Physical delivery of the UHD Dolby Vision Master as a 16-bit DPX, EXR or TIFF longplay sequence (TIFF preferred) written in LTFS format on LTO7 or LTO8. This must include textless backgrounds to the main and end titles of the Picture, and anywhere else text appears over Picture. Dolby Vision Sidecar Metadata XML must also be provided. Picture archival deliveries must include a checksum at the same level as the picture file sequence.
Digital Video Archival Master
Physical delivery of all QC approved master ProRes QuickTime files on LTO7 or LTO8. Picture archival deliveries must include a checksum at the same level as the picture file sequence.
